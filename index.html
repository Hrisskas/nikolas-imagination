The artist

A line of
his own.

Nikolas was born in 2021 and has drawn ever since he could hold a pencil. He has filled hundreds of sketchbooks, most often with a black pencil and without looking at any reference.

Colour appears rarely — and always on his own terms. The Nikolas Imagination logo was drawn entirely by him.
